Skills were never meant to be locked behind a price tag.

SkillSwap started as a simple question: why pay for a course when the person sitting three seats away already knows what you want to learn? We built a place where knowledge moves both ways — no fees, no middlemen, just people teaching each other.

Our Mission

To make learning a two-way exchange instead of a one-way transaction — where every member is both a student and a teacher, and every skill learned opens the door to one taught in return.
